MacNotch turns the notch on your MacBook into a Dynamic Island.

**Install:** download `MacNotch.dmg` below, open it, and drag MacNotch into Applications. The README has the full step-by-step guide, including how to open it the first time.

- Live Activities beside the notch: Apple Music with artwork and sound bars, charging and low battery alerts
- Hover to open a Liquid Glass island with widgets: Now Playing, Weather, Web Pages and a File Shelf
- Add, remove and reorder widgets and web pages in Settings
- Classic Black style and a Clear-to-Tinted glass slider
